MODEL. Qwen3.5-0.8B (text part): 24 decoder layers of width 1,024. Layers 3, 7, 11, 15, 19 and 23 are full (gated) softmax attention (self_attn.q/k/v/o_proj, o_proj 1024 x 2048); the other 18 are linear attention (Gated DeltaNet: linear_attn.in_proj_*, a short convolution, an internal gated norm, linear_attn.out_proj 1024 x 2048). SwiGLU MLPs of width 3,584 (mlp.gate_proj, up_proj, down_proj 1024 x 3584). PRE-NORM: input_layernorm before attention and post_attention_layernorm before the MLP; sublayer outputs are added to the residual stream directly (no norm on the outputs). Final model.norm and a tied 248,320-token embedding.
Weights (bf16, safetensors, read-only): model.safetensors (in your workspace). A linear module computes y = W x with W of shape (d_out, d_in).
