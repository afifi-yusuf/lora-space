LoRA-Space Bench: A Benchmark for Editing Models by Writing Their Weights

An editor model gets a frozen model and one sentence from a user. It has to find what goes wrong and write a LoRA adapter by hand, without gradients. We score the edit on held-out problems and count what it cost.
