(30{,}720 tokens, thinking mode) through the exact edit, its rank-1 LoRA, and the base model (\cref{tab:equiv}).

The LoRA's deviation is ${\sim}39\times$ smaller than the edit's own effect; the residual is bf16 rounding.
Under the paper's sampling protocol the rank-1 LoRA scores 51.2\% on MATH-500 (500 problems $\times$ 4 samples), against the 48.1\% the paper reports for the exact edit.
