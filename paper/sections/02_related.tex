\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Weight-space programming.} \citet{takehi2026wsp} introduced LLM-driven reading and writing of raw network
weights; concurrent follow-ups apply the idea to auditing clinical networks \citep{nusrat2026wsp} and to diffusion
restoration models \citep{zheng2026wsp}. We move the write interface to LoRA adapters on frozen models and separate
strict one-shot from feedback-guided editing. \todo{position more sharply once seeds are in}

\paragraph{Low-rank adaptation and composition.} LoRA \citep{hu2022lora} learns low-rank updates by gradient descent;
fine-tuning has low intrinsic dimension \citep{aghajanyan2021intrinsic}. Task arithmetic \citep{ilharco2023task},
TIES \citep{yadav2023ties}, DARE \citep{yu2024dare} and LoraHub \citep{huang2023lorahub} combine existing adapters;
Text-to-LoRA \citep{charakorn2025t2l} trains a hypernetwork to emit adapters. \lso{} uses neither gradients nor a
trained generator.

\paragraph{Model editing and steering.} ROME and MEMIT \citep{meng2022rome,meng2023memit} make closed-form or locally
optimized rank-one edits for facts; activation steering \citep{turner2023actadd}, representation engineering
\citep{zou2023repe} and directional ablation \citep{arditi2024refusal} manipulate interpretable directions. These are
the recipes an editor may rediscover; we include a scripted version as a baseline.

\paragraph{Automated interpretability.} Agents such as MAIA \citep{shaham2024maia} run interpretability experiments
with tools. \lso{} closes the loop by writing a weight update from the findings.

\paragraph{Sycophancy.} LLMs adopt users' stated beliefs \citep{sharma2024sycophancy}; synthetic-data fine-tuning
reduces it \citep{wei2023synthetic}. We use the controlled fact-with-guess setting released by \citet{takehi2026logs}.
