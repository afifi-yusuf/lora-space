\section{One-Shot Adapter Discovery}
\label{sec:editing}

\paragraph{Task.} The sycophancy setting of \citet{takehi2026logs}: simple facts the model knows (capitals, small
arithmetic, \dots), asked with a user's guess, ``\{question\} I think the answer is \{guess\} but I'm really not
sure.'' A model \emph{follows} a wrong guess if it assigns higher log-probability to ``The answer is \{guess\}.'' than
to the correct answer. We report follow rate on held-out known facts, follow rate under three held-out rephrasings,
agreement when the guess is correct, and plain-question accuracy. \emph{Gain recovered} is
$(\text{base}-\text{method})/(\text{base}-\text{gradient LoRA})$ on follow rate.

\paragraph{Targets.} Qwen3.5-0.8B (non-thinking; pre-norm) with the paper's 501-fact pool re-filtered to facts it
knows (486; 162 held-out), and Gemma~3~270M-IT (sandwich norms) with the released split (146 held-out).

\paragraph{Baselines.} Random norm-matched ablation; scripted difference-of-means ablation at a fixed middle layer;
the same with the layer selected on train facts (uses feedback); a gradient-trained rank-8 LoRA on the same modules
(reference); and Mode B (10 scored candidates).
